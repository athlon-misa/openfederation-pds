Device signing, losing and replacing the device (#240, phase 4), in `@open-federation/sdk/custody`. `rememberDeviceKeyWithPasskey({ privateKey, rpName, userName, credentialId? })` / `unlockDeviceKeyWithPasskey(stored)` keep the device repo key on the device under a passkey (the same WebAuthn PRF sealing as the remembered phrase, with its own labels; `credentialId` reuses the passkey that unlocks the phrase, one prompt). `setDeviceRepoKey` and `disableDeviceSigning` take either the phrase's `rotationPrivateKey` or the recovery key's `recoveryPrivateKey` (#238; then the result has `cancelUntil`, the end of the 72 hours in which the phrase can cancel it), and `setDeviceRepoKey` takes a `client` to tell the PDS. `rotateDeviceRepoKey` makes a new device key and points the DID at it. `recoverWithRecoveryKey` takes `newDeviceDidKey` for a device lost with the phrase. `findDeviceSigningSwitchBack` reads PLC's audit log for a change of the `atproto` key signed by a lower key (the server's backup in `assisted`) and `cancelDeviceSigningSwitchBack` cancels it with the phrase and turns device signing back on. `verifyPreparedCommit` / `deviceApplyWrites` take `trustedHeadKeys`: the device then refuses (`DeviceCommitRejected`, `reason: 'UntrustedHead'`) to sign on top of a head it did not sign. `getDeviceSigningStatus(client)` reads `net.openfederation.account.getDeviceSigning`. Types `AtprotoKeySigner`, `AtprotoKeyChange`, `AtprotoKeySwitch`, `RememberedDeviceKey`, `DeviceSigningStatus`, `DeviceSigningFeature`.
