Device signing, phase 2 (#240), in `@open-federation/sdk/custody`: the features that write on the person's behalf, signed with the device key. `deviceAction({ client, did, deviceKey, verifyAgainst, method, input, expect? })` runs a method through `net.openfederation.repo.prepareAction`, checks what the server plans to write (a commit's new tree with `verifyPreparedCommit`, or a private-community record to sign as its author, its CID recomputed on the device), signs and submits it; it retries on `RepoHeadMoved` and `ActionChanged`. Helpers that also check the planned records say what was asked for: `deviceUpdateProfile`, `deviceCreateThread`, `deviceCreatePost`, `deviceDeletePost`, `deviceRsvp`, `deviceVote`, `deviceObject`, `deviceSendContactRequest`, `deviceRespondToContactRequest`, `deviceRemoveContact`, `deviceWithdrawContactRequest`, `deviceBlockContact`, `deviceUnblockContact`. Tokens: `createServiceAuthToken({ did, deviceKey, aud, lxm?, exp? })` mints an AT Protocol service-auth JWT on the device, and `deviceSignInAssert(...)` signs the SIWOF didToken the PDS prepared after checking its claims against the message. Also exported: `signAuthorRecord`, `signJwtOnDevice`, `cidBytes`, `expectOneRecord`, `expectDeletes`, the collection constants, and the types `DeviceActionRequest`, `DeviceActionResult`, `DeviceActionOptions`, `PlannedWrite`.
