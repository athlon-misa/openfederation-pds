    01 · Elena's app gets ready

    Every month, her app quietly collects 20 passes.

    Each pass lets her create one anonymous name. The server stamps them without seeing them, so it can never recognise them later. Every reporter's app collects them, whether or not they ever use one.

      03 · Look inside the server

      What does the server know?

      Straight from the server's database, not a slide.

      Handle
      The readable name, like a username: nightdesk. It can be changed later.
      DID
      The permanent ID behind the name, like a passport number: did:plc:…. It never changes, so posts and proofs stay tied to it even if the handle changes.
      Public key
      The lock that matches Night Desk's twelve words. The server keeps the lock, never the words, so it can check a login but can't log in as Night Desk.

      04 · Log in and publish

      Twelve words. That's the whole login.

      Night Desk signs in with the words alone, then publishes. A fingerprint of the article is kept in Night Desk's account, so it can't quietly be changed later.

      05 · Prove Night Desk is Elena

      Months later, Elena says: Night Desk is me.

      When Night Desk was created, a sealed note naming Elena was locked inside its public entry. Nobody could read it. Now Elena hands over the opener, and anyone can check the proof: an editor, a court, the public.

      The proofnot made yet

      1. Night Desk signs “Night Desk is Elena Petrova” with its twelve words.
      2. Elena's account signs the same sentence.
      3. The opener for the sealed note inside Night Desk.

        Today Elena's half is signed by her server on her behalf. With self-custody, coming next, only Elena's own key can sign it.

        06 · Prove it face to face

        No file, no record. Just proof, in the room.

        Sometimes Elena can't publish anything. She can still show one person that she holds Night Desk's twelve words.

        Why a word from the audience? A signature made yesterday could have been copied. A word picked right now can only be signed by someone holding Night Desk's words right now.

        • An anonymous name, created in seconds, that the server can't connect to Elena.
        • A login with twelve words and nothing else.
        • A proof, made only when Elena chooses, that Night Desk is her.
        • A face-to-face proof that leaves nothing behind.
